Embrace: towers A and B are one joined building facing the main road, D and E a second joined building at the back, and tower C stands between them on the side, as on Adani Realty’s master layout. Homes: 112 in A+B, 112 in D+E and 24 in C (Gujarat RERA), i.e. 4 per floor on floors 1–14 in A, B, D, E; C has 2 homes per floor on floors 1–11 and 2 duplex penthouses on floors 12–13 (Adani Realty floor plans). Adani Realty describes the towers as “13 and 14 floors”; the floor counts here are those that hold the registered homes. Each flat is where Adani Realty’s typical-floor plans place it, labelled with the number printed at its door (1–4 in A, B, D, E round the lift lobby and the open void between each pair; 1 and 2 in C), which are the last two digits of the Gujarat RERA unit numbers (A-101 … E-1404, C-101 … C-1102). Building outlines: the developer’s official master plan and typical-floor plans, georeferenced on current satellite imagery (each outline is placed where the tower stands today). Plot: the boundary registered on the Gujarat RERA map service. Surroundings (roads, water, parks, other buildings) are from OpenStreetMap; finishes are simplified.
