    Life at Shantigram

    Everything below is already part of the township, according to Adani Realty.

    Leisure & sport

    • The Belvedere Golf & Country Club
    • 9-hole golf course and 1.5 km golf promenade
    • Cricket & football academy with an international-standard ground
    • Swimming pools, tennis courts and gyms

    Nature

    • 7-acre Waterlily Lake with musical aqua show
    • 30,000+ trees and tree-lined avenues
    • Landscaped gardens and bird-watching
    • Shiv Temple and Jain Derasar

    Everyday essentials

    • Adani International School
    • Healthcare centre within the township
    • Shoppers' Plaza with SBI, Axis and Yes Bank branches
    • Sub-post office with its own pin code

    Green mobility & utilities

    • 200+ MYBYK bicycles at 21 locations
    • Electric AC e-bus to Prahlad Nagar
    • Two EV charging stations, fibre-to-the-home
    • Narmada water supply, 24x7 CCTV and response team
