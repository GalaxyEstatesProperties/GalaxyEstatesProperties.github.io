Inside Antonia

Building at a glance

  • Ground floor: a 46'2" × 45'10" entrance foyer with a coffee bar and lounge, a drop-off on the driveway that runs round the tower, and the BMS, fire command and meter rooms.
  • Showroom floors 1 and 2: four showrooms per floor (1,210 – 2,576 sq.ft. super built-up), 4.275 m floor to floor, with a 6'0" passage along the S.G. Highway front.
  • Office floors 3 to 20 and 22 to 31: five offices per floor (1,400 – 2,479 sq.ft.), each with its own toilet, off a 12'6" foyer.
  • Office floors 32 to 42: three large offices per floor (2,479 – 3,234 sq.ft.) with taller 3.65 m floor-to-floor heights.
  • Floor 21 is a skip (refuge) floor. Six lifts serve every office floor, with two pressurised staircases.
  • A landscaped common open plot with pergola seating, a 6 m ramp to four basement parking levels, and entry and exit from the 60 m S.G. Highway.
