    Why GIFT City

    A planned business district with its own financial regulator, built from the ground up next to Ahmedabad.

    0Acres in totalAbout 261 acres SEZ, 625 acres domestic tariff area
    1stInternational Financial Services Centre in IndiaGIFT IFSC

    Sources: GIFT City and GIFT One Tower entries on Wikipedia. Figures change; ask us for the latest.

    Built different

    Smart-city infrastructure

    The services most cities retrofit were planned into GIFT City before the first tower went up.

    District cooling

    Centralised cooling for buildings, run by a dedicated GIFT City subsidiary.

    Waste management

    Solid waste handled by its own company, Gift Waste Management Services.

    Airport access

    About 12 km from Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel International Airport in Ahmedabad.

    Metro connection

    The GIFT City metro station links the district to the Ahmedabad Metro network.
