Vida: two staggered towers joined by a garden bridge, as on the key plan Nila Spaces prints on its unit plans, each with two rows of four homes either side of a central lift and stair spine (8 homes a floor per tower, as in the Gujarat RERA unit list: 16 a floor on floors 1 to 33, none on floors 13 and 21; 496 homes). The site is the block on the north side of the road opposite the petrol pump, as in the address on the listings (“Block No. 46, opp. petrol pump”), where the two towers stand in current imagery. Nila publishes the key plan only as a small inset, so the tower sizes (about 24 × 37 m) are scaled from the registered homes and the split of each floor into flats and which RERA number sits where are indicative. Floor heights are not published: 3.2 m floor to floor is assumed. Surroundings are from OpenStreetMap.
