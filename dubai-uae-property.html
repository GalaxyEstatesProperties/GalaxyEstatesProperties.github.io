Dubai, UAE

Indians were the largest group of buyers in Dubai in 2025. Rent is not taxed in the UAE, a property worth AED 2 million can lead to a 10-year Golden Visa, and off-plan payments go into escrow accounts regulated by the Dubai Land Department.

For Indian investors

Why buy in Dubai

Rent you keep

The UAE has no personal income tax, so rent and resale gains are not taxed there. As a resident Indian you still declare them in India, with no foreign tax to credit.

A 10-year Golden Visa

Property worth AED 2 million or more, alone or combined, qualifies. Off-plan homes in DLD-approved projects count, and since early 2026 mortgaged homes qualify with a bank NOC.

Escrow-protected off-plan

Payments for off-plan homes go into project escrow accounts registered with the Dubai Land Department, and every project’s construction progress is public on the DLD register.

Close to home

Dubai is about three hours by air from Ahmedabad and Mumbai, with daily direct flights, so site visits and handovers are easy.

Deep rental demand

Average gross yields on apartments were 5.53% in May 2026, from 7.80% for studios to 3.76% for 4+ bedroom homes (Global Property Guide). Net yields are usually 1.5 to 2 points lower.

Payment plans

Most off-plan towers are sold on staged plans (for example 20% on booking, 50% during construction, 30% on handover), so the LRS remittances can be spread over several financial years.

From India, step by step

How to buy in Dubai

The Indian rules are the same for every country; the local step is specific to Dubai.

  1. 1

    Send the money under LRS

    Each resident Indian can remit up to USD 250,000 a financial year under the RBI’s Liberalised Remittance Scheme, and buying property abroad is a permitted purpose. Family members can pool their limits for one property only if each is a co-owner of it. HSBC India LRS FAQ

  2. 2

    Plan for TCS at the bank

    Banks collect 20% TCS on LRS remittances above ₹10 lakh in a financial year (other than education and medical). It is not an extra cost: you claim it back as a credit against your income tax or as a refund. RBL Bank, TCS on LRS (revised 1 Apr 2026)

  3. 3

    Buy under local rules

    Buy as a foreigner in a freehold area. You sign a sale agreement, register it with the DLD (Oqood for off-plan) and pay the 4% DLD transfer fee. The title deed is issued in your name; for off-plan, check the project’s DLD number and escrow account first.

  4. 4

    Report it in your Indian return

    Resident Indians are taxed in India on worldwide income, so rent and sale gains abroad go in your Indian return, with credit for tax paid abroad under the tax treaty. The property itself must be shown in Schedule FA (foreign assets) every year. We work with your CA on this.

Questions

Buying in Dubai: common questions

Yes. Foreigners, including resident Indians, can buy freehold property in designated areas of Dubai. The money goes from India under the Liberalised Remittance Scheme, up to USD 250,000 per person per financial year.

Property worth AED 2 million or more can qualify for the 10-year Golden Visa. Off-plan homes in DLD-approved projects count, and mortgaged homes qualify with a bank NOC. A 2-year investor visa is available for completed homes with a title deed.

For a resident Indian, yes: rent from Dubai is part of your worldwide income and is taxed in India. The UAE does not tax it, so there is no foreign tax credit. Declare the property in Schedule FA of your return.

Search its project number on the Dubai Land Department’s Project Status Enquiry. It shows the developer, the registered units, construction progress and the escrow account. Every Dubai project on our site lists its DLD number.
