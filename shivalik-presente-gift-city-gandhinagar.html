Shivalik Présenté: one tower (3 basements + ground + 22, Housiey) on the riverfront in GIFT City, not three: the Gujarat RERA “blocks” A, B and C are the three residences on each floor. The tower is drawn as three staggered wings, one residence each, as on the Shivalik renders, over the ground-floor plan fitted to the plot registered on the RERA map service (within about 6 m). Floors and unit numbers are from the RERA list: homes on floors 2–15 and 17–19 (16 is the refuge floor) and three 6 BHK duplex penthouses over floors 20–21. Typical floor plates are not yet published, so each wing is sized from the registered carpet areas; which wing carries A, B or C is shown north to south. Surroundings (roads, water, parks, other buildings) are from OpenStreetMap; finishes are simplified.
