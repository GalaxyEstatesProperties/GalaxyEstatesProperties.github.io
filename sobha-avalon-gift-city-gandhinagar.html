SOBHA Avalon: one tower on Shram Marg, GIFT City, with two mirror-image wings of five homes each round a shared lift lobby, from the key plans in SOBHA’s floor-plan brochure: type A at the outer corner on the road side, B at the outer corner at the back, E and D along the back, and C on the road side next to the lobby (unit number’s last digit 1 = C, 2 = A, 3 = B, 4 = E, 5 = D). The first digit of the unit number (1 or 2) is the wing; SOBHA does not publish which wing is which, so wing 1 is drawn on the north-east side. The plan is scaled from the unit areas on SOBHA’s unit plans and placed on the construction site seen in satellite imagery, its long side along the road; the registered plot outline on the Gujarat RERA map lies about 30 m to the south-west of the site and is not used. Floors: 2 basements, ground floor with the clubhouse, 3 podium parking levels, and homes on floors 5 to 31 as in the Gujarat RERA unit list (floor 4 has no home registered). Floor heights are not published: 3.2 m floor to floor is assumed. Surroundings are from OpenStreetMap.
