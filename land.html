How it works

How a land deal runs

The same six steps apply whether you are buying or selling.

  1. Tell us what you need

    Buy or sell, the district, the size or budget, and what the land is for. A call or a WhatsApp message is enough.

  2. Shortlist or prepare

    Buyers get plots with a map pin and photos. Sellers get a short list of documents to gather.

  3. Documents first

    7/12 and 8A extracts, Village Form 6, the NA order, tenure and the Jantri value are reviewed before any token is paid. Your advocate gives the final title opinion.

  4. Visit the land

    Walk the boundary, check the access road and confirm the measurement against the records.

  5. Agree and pay

    Price, payment schedule and conditions go into a written agreement. Pay through banking channels.

  6. Register and update records

    The sale deed is registered at the sub-registrar's office, then the mutation entry is made so the 7/12 shows the new owner.

Before you pay

What to check on any land in Gujarat

These six checks catch most problems early. Your advocate makes the final call.

7/12 and 8A extracts

Ownership, survey number, area, crop and any loan or court entry (bojo). You can view them free online.

AnyROR portal

Farmer or non-farmer land

Farm land can generally be bought only by a verified farmer. A non-farmer needs the Collector's permission under Section 63 of the Bombay Tenancy and Agricultural Lands Act, which is rarely given.

Revenue office · your advocate

NA permission

Land used for homes, factories or shops needs non-agricultural (NA) permission under Section 65 of the Gujarat Land Revenue Code. Ask for the NA order and the approved layout.

District Collector · i-ORA portal

Old or new tenure

New tenure land needs state permission and a premium before it can be sold or converted. Check the tenure remark on the 7/12.

7/12 remark · Collector's office

Title and heirs

Every co-owner and legal heir must sign the sale deed. An advocate should search the title and encumbrances before you pay a token.

Your advocate · sub-registrar records

Jantri value and duty

Duty is charged on the higher of the deal price and the Jantri (government) value. We last noted 4.9% stamp duty plus 1% registration. Confirm the current rates before you sign.

GARVI portal · sub-registrar

General information, not legal advice. Land rules, circulars and duty rates change. Always get a title opinion from an advocate and confirm the latest rules with the revenue office and the sub-registrar before you pay.
