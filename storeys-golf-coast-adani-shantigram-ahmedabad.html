The Storeys Golf Coast: two towers on a shared podium beside the Narmada canal and the Belvedere golf course. The North tower (RERA block A) holds the 5 BHK duplexes and faces the canal; the South tower (block B) has one 4 BHK simplex on each floor. Tower outlines and the 22 m gap between them are measured on current satellite imagery (the towers are standing; roofs are shifted back for the camera’s lean) and agree with Adani Realty’s site and podium plans; floors from the RERA unit numbers (homes on floors 2 to 20 above the entrance level and the podium amenity floor; the South tower’s top home, B-19, is larger than a whole floor and is drawn over floors 19 and 20). Floor heights are from the Ahmedabad fire-safety opinion filed on Gujarat RERA (the same for both towers): ground floor 4.65 m, floor 1 4.45 m and 3.45 m for each of floors 2 to 20, 74.65 m from ground level to the top slab.
