Model built from the developer’s brochure for Stella (floor plans for every floor from the basements to the terrace, drawn to scale). Building form: three towers on a shared podium, bent to follow the plot: Skye (tower A, north) and Sygnus (tower C, south) rise to the 33rd floor, Sol (tower B, middle) to the 20th; the 21st floor is the skip floor, with an enclosed safe corridor across Sol’s roof linking Skye and Sygnus. The ground and 1st floors hold 24 shops on the river-front road with parking behind; the 2nd floor is the amenity floor, with the pool and river-view deck on the podium roof. Footprints are traced from the plans; the planted bands, roof gardens and the Bakeri sign follow the developer’s renders. Height: 118.85 m to the terrace (fire-safety opinion filed on Gujarat RERA); floor-to-floor heights are estimated (4.75 m ground, 4.5 m 1st, 4.2 m 2nd, 3.4 m above). Position and orientation: fitted to the site on current satellite imagery. Unit numbers and carpet areas are from the Gujarat RERA register; where each numbered home sits on its floor is indicative.
