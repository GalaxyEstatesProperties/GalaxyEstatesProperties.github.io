Inspire Corporate Capital: two glass office blocks joined by a low two-storey wing with a landscaped roof, a curved drop-off canopy and a courtyard, on the 8,603.72 sq m plot registered on the Gujarat RERA map service. Block outlines are traced on current satellite imagery of the site (together about 3,700 sq m on the ground, matching the 3,724 sq m registered as covered area). Floors: ground + 11 in each block, counted on Adani Realty’s renders and its August site photos (no floor-wise unit list is filed on RERA); 11’6” internal floor height. The recessed terraces at the top of each block follow the renders. RERA registers 43 units in block A and 62 in block B but does not say which block is which on the plot, so they are named north and south here. Surroundings (roads, water, parks, other buildings) are from OpenStreetMap; finishes are simplified.
