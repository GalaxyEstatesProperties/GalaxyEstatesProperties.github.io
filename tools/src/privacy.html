Who receives it

  • Only our team, through services that process it for us: FormSubmit (delivers forms to our email), Google (email, the land-listing sheet and, if allowed, Analytics) and GitHub (hosts this website), and, only if you tick the optional box, ipapi.co or ipwho.is, which see your internet address to work out your city. We do not keep your internet address. Some of these may store data outside India.

  • Where the law requires, for example to a court or government authority.

We never sell your data or share it for anyone else’s marketing, and we do not upload your name, number or email to any advertising platform.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries: deleted one year after our last contact with you, unless you buy, sell or lease through us; then the deal records are kept only as long as tax and RERA laws require.
  • Land listings: removed when the land is sold or when you ask, and deleted within 30 days.
  • On your own device: a form sent while you are offline waits in your browser and is removed once it is delivered; a half-filled land form is kept in your browser for 14 days.
  • Google Analytics: visit data is kept for at most 14 months.

Your rights

You can ask us for a summary of your personal data and who we shared it with, to correct, complete or update it, to erase it, and to nominate a person to exercise these rights if you die or cannot act yourself. Contact us as shown above; we may check that the request comes from the same mobile number or email. We acknowledge every request within 48 hours and resolve it within 30 days.

Complaints

Please write to us first, so we can put it right within the time above. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Security and breaches

The website runs only over HTTPS, forms go straight to our mailbox, and only our team can open it. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you without delay what happened, its likely effect and what you can do, and report it to the Data Protection Board as the Rules require.

Children

This website is for adults. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to contact us. If we find we have a child’s details without a parent’s verifiable consent, we delete them. We do not track children or show them targeted advertising.

Changes

We will update the date above when this notice changes, and ask for your consent again before using your data for any new purpose.
