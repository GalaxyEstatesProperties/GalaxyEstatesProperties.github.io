SOBHA Elysia: two towers on 2.65 acres in GIFT City, tower 2 to the west and tower 1 to the east, joined between their north wings by a lower bridge block that holds the 12 duplexes (floors 6 to 17) and the Sky Lounge on floor 18, as on SOBHA’s master plan, key plans and renders. Each tower’s outline, lift lobby and its eight homes per floor are cut from the key plan in SOBHA’s floor-plan brochure; the positions of unit types A, D and J and the duplex F are marked on those key plans, and the other homes follow the unit numbering round the core (tower 1 is drawn as the mirror image of tower 2). The drawing is scaled from the unit areas on SOBHA’s unit plans and fitted on current satellite imagery, where the lower floors of both towers are built (within about 2 m). Floors (SOBHA: 4 basements, 2-level stilt parking, a skip floor and 35 floors of homes): ground floor entrance, clubhouse and parking on floors 1 and 2, homes on floors 3 to 38 as in the Gujarat RERA unit list, with floor 24 the skip floor. Floor heights are not published: 3.2 m floor to floor is assumed, with a double-height entrance lobby. Plot: the site boundary on SOBHA’s master plan (about 10,600 sq m drawn; 10,712 sq m registered). Surroundings are from OpenStreetMap.
