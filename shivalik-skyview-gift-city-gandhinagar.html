Shivalik Skyview: the “drone” tower by INI Design Studio, 120 m and 34 storeys: a central block around an open void with four arms reaching to the corners, traced from Shivalik’s typical-floor plan and placed on the tower now rising on site (current satellite imagery, where the arms and the void are visible; the plot drawn on the RERA map service sits about 40 m to its west). Every floor from 2 to 21 and 23 to 32 has 12 homes, three on each of the lift cores A, B, C and D (numbered x01 at the end of the arm, x02 on the arm and x03 on the central block, as on the developer’s key plans); floor 22 is the skip floor and floors 33–34 hold the 12 duplex penthouses.
