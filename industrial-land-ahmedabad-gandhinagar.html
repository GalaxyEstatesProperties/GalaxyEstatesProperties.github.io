Before you pay

What to check on industrial land

These checks catch most problems early. Your advocate makes the final call.

7/12 and 8A extracts

Ownership, survey number, area and any loan or court entry (bojo). You can view them free online.

AnyROR portal

NA permission and zone

Land used for factories needs non-agricultural (NA) permission under Section 65 of the Gujarat Land Revenue Code. Ask for the NA order and the approved layout, and confirm industrial use is allowed on the plot.

District Collector · your advocate

GIDC or private land

Plots on a GIDC estate are allotted by GIDC under its own rules. Before you buy a plot on resale, ask GIDC about transfer rules, dues and fees.

GIDC regional office

Power, water and road

Confirm the power load, water supply, drainage and a road wide enough for trucks before you pay a token.

Site visit · utility providers

Pollution board consent

Many industries need consent from the Gujarat Pollution Control Board before they build or start. Ask your consultant what applies to your product.

GPCB · your consultant

Title and heirs

Every co-owner and legal heir must sign the sale deed. An advocate should search the title and encumbrances before you pay a token.

Your advocate · sub-registrar records

General information, not legal advice. Land rules, circulars and duty rates change. Get a title opinion from an advocate and confirm the latest rules with the revenue office, GIDC and the sub-registrar before you pay.
