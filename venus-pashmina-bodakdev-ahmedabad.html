Venus Pashmina: two parallel residential slabs on a corner plot at the junction of two 18 m roads in Bodakdev, round a courtyard with the swimming pool, as on Venus’s typical-floor and ground-floor plans (published on the GharPe listing): blocks D and E in the west slab with 2 homes a floor each (D1, D2, E2, E1 on the plan), blocks C, B and A in the east slab with one home a floor each. Homes are on floors 4 to 20 (Gujarat RERA unit list: 126 homes, 7 a floor), with duplexes over floors 21 and 22 (the floor-21 units are about twice the size of the others). Floor heights are from the Ahmedabad fire-safety opinion filed on Gujarat RERA: ground floor and floors 1 and 2 (parking) 3.33 m each, floor 3 (podium for the society’s common use) and floors 4 to 22 3.50 m each, 80 m from ground level to the top slab, over two 4.50 m basements. The plans are fitted to the two slab roofs in current satellite imagery (tall buildings lean in satellite pictures, so within about 5 m). Surroundings are from OpenStreetMap.
