Shilp Twin Towers: two office towers of ground + 28 floors on plots 15-D and 15-E in the GIFT City SEZ, with the club block and a landscaped court between them, from the floor plans on Shilp’s website (INI Design Studio). Every floor group has its own plan, and each one is drawn here as published: the cafés and shops on the ground floor, units 101 to 107 on floor 1, eleven offices on most office floors (ten on floors 4 and 17, which have common terraces), the changing setbacks and terraces of the facade, and the lower terrace on floor 28. Unit numbers are as printed on the plans and match the Gujarat RERA unit list (295 units). Floor 19 has no unit registered. Both towers are numbered alike on Shilp’s plans; the RERA registration (CAA15149) lists one set of 295 units. Scale from the printed office sizes; placed on the two tower structures under construction in current satellite imagery (within about 5 m). Height about 120 m (INI Design Studio), so about 4.1 m floor to floor. Surroundings are from OpenStreetMap.
