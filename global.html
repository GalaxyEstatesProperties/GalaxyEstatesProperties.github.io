Middle East & Africa Premium

Dubai

United Arab Emirates · Downtown, Dubai Marina, Palm Jumeirah, Dubai Hills

Tax-free rental income, payment plans and a 10-year visa route make Dubai the first stop for most Indian investors.

  • Rental income
  • Residency
  • Off-plan

Why investors buy

  • No personal income tax in the UAE on rent or resale gains
  • 10-year renewable Golden Visa with AED 2 million of property; off-plan and mortgaged homes can count
  • Off-plan payments go into regulated escrow accounts, often spread over construction

Properties we source

  • Branded waterfront apartments
  • Off-plan towers on payment plans
  • Palm and golf-course villas
Ownership rules and developers

Foreigners can buy freehold in designated areas. The Dubai Land Department charges a 4% transfer fee.

Why buy abroad

What overseas property adds to a portfolio

Strong-currency assets

Rent and resale value in dirhams, pounds, euros or Singapore dollars balance wealth held in rupees.

Residency routes

Dubai’s 10-year Golden Visa starts at AED 2 million of property. Other markets have their own long-stay options.

Rental income

Holiday lets in Phuket and Bali, long lets in London and Berlin, serviced apartments in Dubai.

Education and family

A home near your children’s university in London or Singapore, which you can let after they graduate.

Low-tax locations

The UAE and Monaco charge no personal income tax. Residents of India still declare and pay tax here, with credit for tax paid abroad.

Payment plans

Off-plan projects in Dubai and Southeast Asia spread payments over construction instead of one lump sum.

From India to the keys

How an overseas purchase works with us

  1. Brief

    Your budget, goal (income, growth, visa or a holiday home) and the markets you are drawn to.

  2. Shortlist

    Three to five properties with price sheets, video walkthroughs and the numbers for each.

  3. Visit

    See the property and the neighbourhood in person. When your purchase completes through us, we refund your travel cost for the visit.

  4. Due diligence

    Developer registration, title, escrow for off-plan units, service charges and the local taxes you will pay.

  5. Remit and sign

    Your bank sends the money under LRS with Form A2. We coordinate the booking and the local lawyer.

  6. Handover and letting

    Snagging, furnishing, a rental manager if you want one, and the papers your CA needs for Schedule FA.

Good to know

Before you invest abroad

Yes. Under the RBI’s Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS) each resident individual can send up to USD 250,000 abroad per financial year, through an authorised bank, and this can be used to buy property. Family members buying together can each use their own limit. Your bank will ask for the sale agreement and Form A2. Confirm the current limit and paperwork with your bank before you sign.

If you are resident in India for tax, your worldwide income is taxable here. Tax paid in the other country can usually be credited under the double taxation agreement between India and that country. Overseas property must also be declared in Schedule FA of your income tax return. Speak to your chartered accountant before you buy.

Banks collect Tax Collected at Source on LRS remittances above a yearly threshold. It is not an extra cost: you can adjust it against your income tax or claim it back in your return. Rates and thresholds change with each budget, so check the current figures with your bank.

In some places. Dubai gives a 10-year Golden Visa for AED 2 million of property. Georgia and Malaysia have residence routes linked to property or long stays, and Monaco asks for a home there before granting residence. Thailand does not give a visa for buying property. Rules change, so we confirm the current position for your market.
