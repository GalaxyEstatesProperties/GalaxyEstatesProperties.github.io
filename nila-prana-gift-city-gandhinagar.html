Prana: one L-shaped residential building, drawn from the floor-plan modules on Nila Spaces’ website: a west wing with 12 homes a floor and a south wing with 16, which match the Gujarat RERA unit list’s towers A (12 a floor) and B (16 a floor), joined by the lift and stair core and a double-height community space in the inner corner. Each flat is drawn between the shear walls and wet cores shown on the plans. The building is placed with the plans’ west site line on the basement wall in current imagery. Floor heights are not published: 3.2 m floor to floor is assumed. Surroundings are from OpenStreetMap.
