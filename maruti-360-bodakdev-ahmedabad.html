Maruti 360: two towers on the plot next to Karnavati Club, from the developer’s plans brochure: tower A (east, 4 BHK) and tower B (west, 5 BHK), each with two residences per typical floor (east and west of the lift lobby, verandahs facing south), the lift and stair core on the road side, and the podium with pool, garden and clubhouse behind. The site plan is fitted to the plot walls in satellite imagery (north edge on the road, east edge on the S.G. Highway service road) and agrees with the towers now built. Floors and homes are from the Gujarat RERA unit list: homes on floors 3 to 37, two a floor, none registered on floors 13 and 21, duplexes over floors 34–35 and penthouses over 36–37 (3 basements + ground + 37 floors: the 41 storeys Maruti quotes). Floor heights are from the Ahmedabad fire-safety opinion of 20 Sep 2022 filed on Gujarat RERA: ground floor 6.00 m, floor 1 4.20 m and 3.60 m for every floor above, which puts the roof of floor 37 at 139.80 m; the towers are 153.33 m to the top (that opinion and the environmental clearance; Maruti quotes 501 ft). That opinion was issued for an earlier floor arrangement, so the levels above floor 1 follow its typical height. Which side of each tower carries the 01 and which the 02 numbers is not marked on the published plans. Surroundings are from OpenStreetMap.
