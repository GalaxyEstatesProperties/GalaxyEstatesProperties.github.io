Shilp North Sky: one building of three blocks along the road on the north side of the plot: block B the long bar in the middle, blocks A (east) and C (west) with wings that run south round the podium courtyard with the pool. Each block has 9 homes round its own lift lobby on the typical floor; all flats, lobbies and passages are read off Shilp’s published floor plans (first floor, second floor, typical floors 3 to 24, penthouse floor 25), with flat numbers as printed. Floor 1 holds the Club Luxe in block B and homes in the wings of A and C. The plan is fitted to the roof of the building in current satellite imagery (within about 5 m), its north as on Shilp’s north arrow. Gujarat RERA registers 668 units (224, 223 and 221 in blocks A, B and C), a few more than the published plans show (636), so some floors may differ from the typical plan. The fire-safety opinion for the building (Regional Fire Officer, GIFT City, 21 Mar 2023, filed on Gujarat RERA) gives its height as 90.10 m; floor-by-floor heights are not published, so the 25 floors above a 4.5 m ground floor share that height evenly (about 3.42 m each). Plot: the site boundary on Shilp’s site plan. Surroundings are from OpenStreetMap.
