An engineer by training, Samir founded Galaxy Estates Properties to give buyers and sellers one advisor who checks the paperwork as closely as the price.

Over more than five years he has worked on commercial sales, luxury homes, land and industrial plots, in Gujarat, in other Indian states and with buyers abroad. Every deal starts with the survey, title and tax position, before any money moves.
