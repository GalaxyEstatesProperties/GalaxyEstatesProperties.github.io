Specifications

District
Six-hectare sea extension at Le Portier, Monaco, inaugurated in December 2024
Homes
Le Renzo by Renzo Piano Building Workshop, the residences of Les Jardins d’Eau, a limited number of seaside villas, and townhouses with interior patios
Designers
Master plan by Valode & Pistre Architectes; buildings by Renzo Piano Building Workshop; landscape by Michel Desvigne Paysagiste

800 trees, 27,000 m² of planted areas, 19,000 m² of pedestrian space and 600 m of cycle path
