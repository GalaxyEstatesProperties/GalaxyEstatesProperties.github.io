Trogon Twin Towers: two L-shaped office towers facing each other across a diagonal court, on a retail podium at the corner of S.G. Highway and the 60 m ring road, floor by floor from the plans in Trogon’s brochure: ground + 4 retail levels (24 shops a floor along the highway and the ring road, 7 offices a floor in each tower), level 5 with terrace restaurants (12 units per tower), and work levels 6 to 32 with 13 offices per tower as drawn on the work-level plans (606 and 607 are drawn as one office; Gujarat RERA lists them separately: 928 units in all). No unit is registered on floor 19. Unit numbers are as in the Gujarat RERA unit list. Height: 125 m (Trogon brochure) to the roof (the sloping glass crowns of the renders are not drawn); floor heights are not published floor by floor, so retail levels are drawn at 4.6 m (14′9″ clear, brochure) and office levels at 3.63 m so the roof is at 125 m. The towers are placed on their structures in current satellite imagery (within a few metres). Surroundings are from OpenStreetMap.
