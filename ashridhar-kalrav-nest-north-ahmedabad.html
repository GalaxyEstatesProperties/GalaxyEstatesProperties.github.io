What you get

  • 200 acres at Govindpura in six phases, beside a lake: 319 villa plots
  • Villa plots of about 1,350 – 7,000 sq.yd (size plan: 1,318 – 12,616 sq.yd super built-up)
  • Villas built by the developer on every plot (construction compulsory), in five designs from DPA Design Studio and R+R Architects, 2 to 4 BHK, 280 – 710 sq.yd super built-up
  • New Phoenix series by Studio Eethetics: 2, 3 and 4 BHK in 14 ft, 16 ft and 18 ft versions, 377 – 603 sq.yd built-up
  • Clubhouse with JOMO Cafe, swimming pool, gym, café, lounge, multipurpose hall, mini theatre and indoor games
  • Pickleball court, divine court and seven brooks: forest brook walk, stepping stone, fruit orchard, nest of paths, lily pond, pocket and island brooks
  • Possession Dec 2026 (developer)
