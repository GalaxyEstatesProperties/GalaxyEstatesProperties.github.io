    Finding the right property in Ahmedabad and Gandhinagar

    From our office in Kalol we cover new projects, resale and land across the twin cities. Here is where each kind of property is, and the projects we can show you now.

    Luxury flats in Ahmedabad

    Large 4 and 5 BHK homes are concentrated along S.G. Highway: Maruti 360, Anamika High Point, Ikebana, Venus Pashmina and The Banyan in Bodakdev, Godrej Altus in Vastrapur, The Universe by Venus at Nehru Nagar, and Trogon Centre Court on S.P. Ring Road near Vaishnodevi Circle.

    Luxury properties in Shantigram

    Adani Shantigram is a gated township between Ahmedabad and Gandhinagar with a golf course and a lake. Compare Belrosa, Skyline and Ambrosia, or villas at The North Park, in the 3D township map.

    Offices and showrooms on S.G. Highway

    Vaishnodevi Circle is the city's newest business address, with Shivalik Wave, Trogon Twin Towers and the upcoming Antonia; Shilp Business Gateway near Nirma University; on Sindhu Bhavan Road, Wynn; at Nehru Nagar, Stratum @ Venus Grounds. At Iscon Cross Road, Navratna Gulmohar Park combines offices, shops and homes.

    New projects in GIFT City

    Homes near India's first international financial centre: Sobha Elysia, Shivalik Présenté, Savvy Marina, Bakeri Stella, Shivalik Skyview, Shilp North Sky and Vida by Nila Spaces, with prices and RERA numbers. See the GIFT City 3D map.

    Weekend homes, plots and land

    Weekend villas and plots near Thol, Kadi and Sanand: the Kalrav series by A. Shridhar (Kalrav Seasons, Kalrav Nest, Kalrav Farms, Kalrav Trails and Kalrav Treasure; the plot projects have a 3D map of every plot), Arvind Aqua City, Suryam Repose and Shivalik Farmland (all weekend homes). We also handle residential plots, farm land, industrial and commercial land in all 34 districts, with title and document checks before you pay. See land for sale in Gujarat.

    Property abroad for Indian buyers

    Dubai, Singapore, Monaco, Phuket, Pattaya and Kuala Lumpur, from developers such as Emaar, Binghatti, Danube and CDL. Start with Burj Binghatti Jacob & Co Residences, Bayz 101 by Danube and Valo, or browse all markets.

    Questions buyers ask us

    Which are the best areas to buy property in Ahmedabad?

    For luxury flats, buyers look most at S.G. Highway, Bodakdev, Sindhu Bhavan Road and Adani Shantigram. For offices and showrooms, Vaishnodevi Circle on S.G. Highway and Iscon Cross Road. GIFT City in Gandhinagar suits buyers who want to be close to the financial centre.

    Are these projects RERA registered?

    Every listing shows its Gujarat RERA number, and we check it on the GujRERA portal before you book. Where a developer has not published the number (Antonia, some weekend-plot projects and pre-launches), the listing says so and we confirm the registration with you before any booking.

    Where can I buy a weekend home or plot near Ahmedabad?

    Most weekend villas and plots are around Thol, Kadi Road and Sanand, 30 to 60 minutes from S.G. Highway: the Kalrav series by A. Shridhar (Kalrav Seasons, Kalrav Nest, Kalrav Farms, Kalrav Trails and Kalrav Treasure), Arvind Aqua City and Arvind Uplands 3.0, Suryam Repose and The Sage, Safal Rethal Greens and Shivalik Farmland. Arvind Forreste in Nasmed is close to Gandhinagar and Kalol, and Kalrav Alpines in Nandoli, near Shilaj, has plots for a home closer to the city.

    What does a 4 BHK flat in Ahmedabad cost?

    On our current list, 4 BHK homes start at about ₹2.23 Cr* at Skyline in Adani Shantigram and ₹2.77 Cr* at Trogon Centre Court. Prices marked * are as published by the developer and change often, so ask us for the latest price sheet.

    Can NRIs buy property in Ahmedabad?

    Yes. NRIs can buy flats, villas, offices and shops in India. Agricultural land, plantations and farmhouses are the exception under FEMA rules. We help with site videos, documents and registration while you are abroad.
