Ikaria: one slab building of ground + 13 floors beside the 34.5 m road, drawn from Adani Realty’s ground-floor, first-floor and typical-floor plans. Floor 1 has 8 homes (101–108) round the amenity hall; floors 2 to 13 have 11 homes each, numbered as on the typical-floor plan (01–05 on the north side, 06–11 on the south side), which adds up to the 140 homes registered on Gujarat RERA. Two lift cores (lifts 1–2 and 3–4), each with its stair. The building is placed with Adani Realty’s master layout, whose plot outline is fitted to the plot registered on Gujarat RERA. Floor heights are not published: 3.2 m floor to floor and a 4.2 m stilt are assumed. Surroundings are from OpenStreetMap.
