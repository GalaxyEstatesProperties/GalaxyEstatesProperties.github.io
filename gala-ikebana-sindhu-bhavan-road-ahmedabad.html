Ikebana (Gala Group): one H-shaped tower with three lift cores, A (east wing, on Sindhu Bhavan Road), B (central bar) and C (west wing). Every floor height is from the Ahmedabad fire-safety opinion filed on Gujarat RERA: ground floor 5.08 m, floor 1 3.90 m, floor 2 (podium) 4.20 m and 3.46 m for each of floors 3 to 21; 87.2 m from ground level to the top slab. Floor uses are from the sanctioned area table: parking on the ground floor and floor 1 (and three basements), the Earth Level amenity podium on floor 2, 7 homes on each of floors 3 to 17 and 14 duplexes on floors 18–21 (119 homes). Flat numbers, balconies, wash yards and the planter ledge are placed as on the RERA carpet-area key plans (odd and even floors differ slightly); tap a flat for its RERA carpet and balcony area. The outline is Gala’s typical-floor plan fitted to the roof in current satellite imagery. Surroundings are from OpenStreetMap.
