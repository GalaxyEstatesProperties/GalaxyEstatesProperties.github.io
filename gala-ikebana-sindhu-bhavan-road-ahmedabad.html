Ikebana (Gala Group): one H-shaped tower, not three: blocks A (east wing, on Sindhu Bhavan Road), B (the central bar) and C (west wing) are its three lift cores. The outline, cores and the seven flats of a typical floor are cut from Gala’s typical-floor plan (flats 01, 02 and 03 off the A and C lobbies, and flat 01 off the B lobby). The plan is fitted to the roof in current satellite imagery and moved to the building’s foot; position within about 3 m. Floors: ground floor with the entrance foyers and parking, floor 1 the Earth Level amenity floor, homes from floor 2 to floor 21 (Gala: 21 floors), and the Sky Level gardens on the roof. Heights are measured on Gala’s drone photo of 8 October 2025, which shows the floor numbers painted on the slabs: 3.27 m floor to floor, and a 13.6 m terracotta base for the ground floor and floor 1. Gujarat RERA registers 119 homes (carpet 308.29 to 628.51 sq m), fewer than 7 on every floor, so some floors hold fewer, larger homes; Gala does not publish those plans, so every home floor is drawn with the typical plan. Plot: the site boundary on Gala’s ground-floor plan. Surroundings are from OpenStreetMap.
