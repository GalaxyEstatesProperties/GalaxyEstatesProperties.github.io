Navratna Gulmohar Park: one stepped mixed-use tower, 161.5 m (RERA) and 39 storeys (INI Design Studio): a four-level retail podium (ground to floor 3), offices on floors 2–15 (floor 9 a refuge floor, floor 16 services), the amenity floor 17 with pool, banquet and gym, homes from floor 18 (two per floor) and a pair of double-height sky villas on top. Floors and unit numbers are from the Gujarat RERA unit list (5 shops, 125 offices, 40 homes) and the uses per level from the architect, INI Design Studio.
